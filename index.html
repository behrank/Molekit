We use this data only in aggregated form to see how the game performs and which modes people enjoy. Analytics data is not used to identify you personally and is not combined with your advertising identifier. More information: Firebase privacy.

"Tracking" here means linking data from this app with data from other companies' apps and websites to show you personalized ads or to measure advertising. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. If you are in these regions, the app shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy options.

Without your consent, only non-personalized or limited ads are shown. These may still use your IP address and device information for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention. Learn how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
